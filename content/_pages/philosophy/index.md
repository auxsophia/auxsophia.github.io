# For the love of wisdom!

<img src="../images/Raphael_School_of_Athens.jpg" width="100%" height="100%">

 [Raphael's The School of Athens](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/The_School_of_Athens)


[Philosophy](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Philosophy) is a complex concept. I believe philosophy is a combination of thinking about thinking and thinking about how our thoughts relate to reality. The three modern major branches as questions are
- What is reality? (_Metaphysics_)
- What is knowledge? (_Epistemology_)
- What are values? (_Value theory/Axiology_)

Giving a small tree of philosophy:

- Metaphysics
  - Ontology
  - Philosophy of Mind
  - Philosophy of Time
- Epistemology
  - Logic
    - Critical Thinking
    - Mathematical Logic
    - Para-consistent Logic
  - Meta-philosophy
    - Meta-ethics
  - Philosophy of History
  - Philosophy of Language
  - Philosophy of Religion
  - Philosophy of Science
- Axiology
  - Aesthetics
  - Ethics
    - Applied Ethics
    - Political Philosophy

[The Internet Encyclopedia of Philosophy (IEP)](https://www.iep.utm.edu/home/about/)

The Encyclopedia receives no funding, and operates through the volunteer work of the editors, authors, volunteers, and technical advisers.

For any philosophical concept I write about, if you want to dig deeper I suggest reading about the idea on [Wikipedia](https://www.wikipedia.org/), [SEP](https://plato.stanford.edu/contents.html), and [IEP](https://www.iep.utm.edu/home/about/), then you'll be an expert ;)
